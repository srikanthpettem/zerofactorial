How it moves

A short path from ambiguity to traction.

    Understand the goal, constraints, current assets, and the work already in motion.

  2. Shape

    Choose the smallest useful direction and define what needs to exist next.

  3. Build

    Create the product, system, or launch surface with enough detail to use immediately.

  4. Refine

    Measure what changed, remove friction, and hand over a clearer operating rhythm.
